The syllabus columns are deliberately left as *to verify*: each topic must be checked against the official Cambridge O Level and NCTB SSC documents before any coverage claim is made.

## How to read this map
- **Grade:** suggested grade at which the topic is first taught. *Draft.*
- **Builds on:** topics a student should meet first, by ID.
- **Status:**
  - **Built:** in the app with interactive exploration.
  - **Partial:** some of it is in the app.
  - **Planned:** not built yet.
- **Proof:** whether the lab has a visual proof that lets the learner watch *why* the result holds (D-020, D-023), not only see *that* it holds. "—" means the topic has no theorem to prove.
- **Lab:** where it lives in the app.
